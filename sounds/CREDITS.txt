Disc drive and keyboard recordings
==================================

These are real recordings, cut and levelled for CPCSyntaxError. Replace any of them with
your own WAV files (same names; numbered variants such as drive_step1.wav, drive_step2.wav
are played in turn). The names the program looks for are listed in machine_sounds.h:

  drive_motor_start.wav, drive_motor.wav (looped), drive_motor_stop.wav,
  drive_step*.wav, drive_insert*.wav, drive_eject*.wav,
  key_press*.wav, key_release*.wav, key_space_press*.wav, key_return_press*.wav

drive_*.wav
  "Amiga 600 floppy drive sounds" by asie
  https://opengameart.org/content/amiga-600-floppy-drive-sounds
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
  The motor loop and the single head steps are cut from its "access" recordings.
