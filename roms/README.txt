Amstrad CPC firmware ROMs, the CPC Plus / GX4000 system cartridge, and the M4 board ROM

  Amstrad CPC 464 OS (1985)(Amstrad).bin
  Amstrad CPC 464 BASIC (1985)(Amstrad).bin
  Amstrad CPC 6128 OS (1985)(Amstrad).bin
  Amstrad CPC 6128 BASIC (1986)(Amstrad).bin
  AMSDOS.ROM
  Basic - Burning Rubber (E) (Yellow Label).cpr   (CPC Plus / GX4000 system cartridge)

Amstrad have kindly given their permission for the redistribution of their
copyrighted material but retain that copyright. The system cartridge also
contains the game Burning Rubber, which remains the copyright of its owners.

  M4ROM.ROM   the M4 board's Z80 ROM (v2.0.6), by Duke -- www.spinpoint.org. Included
              with his permission; it remains his copyright.

Not included: Yamaha's YRW801 sample ROM for the OPL4 card. Turning the OPL4 on
without it opens a prompt to download it from a URL or choose a copy you have; it
is saved here as yrw801.rom.

Not included: General Instrument's SP0256-AL2 internal ROM (2 KB), for the SSA-1,
dk'tronics and LambdaSpeak 3 speech synthesisers. Choosing one without it opens the
same prompt; it is saved here as sp0256-al2.bin.

These are not part of CPCSyntaxError and are not covered by its MIT licence.
cpcse.exe looks for them in the "roms" folder of its working directory
(File > ROM folder... chooses another).
